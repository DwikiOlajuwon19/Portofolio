About

I am a cybersecurity professional who identifies security vulnerabilities in web applications, mobile apps and APIs.

My reports cover what I found, its potential impact, and actionable remediation steps, so development teams can fix issues and make their systems more secure and resilient.

Before moving into security, I spent several years in quality assurance on banking and finance projects. That background shows in how I test: carefully, with evidence, and with a report someone else can follow.
